Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about StudentHub, your account, courses, and academic information.

StudentHub is a centralized student portal for managing courses, attendance, assignments, results, timetable, events, and university notices.

Select Register and complete the registration form using your university information.

Select Forgot Password on the Login page and enter your university email or student ID.

Open Attendance from the Academic Tools section of the student portal.

Open Results from the Academic Tools section to view your grades and academic performance.

Visit the Contact page or use the Help section inside the student portal.
